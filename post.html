How I Learned HTML

In my first year of university I began learning HTML through tutorials and practice projects. Building the first page and seeing it in the browser was very motivating.

I kept experimenting by making small personal sites, and gradually added CSS and JavaScript to improve the interactivity and layout.

Practice and small projects helped more than just reading — try building something small every week!
